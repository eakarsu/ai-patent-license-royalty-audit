# Patent License Royalty Audit

Recover patent royalties through product coverage, net-sales, rate, minimum, sublicense, and territory reconciliation.

**Primary buyer:** Patent owners, licensors, and technology companies. **Evidence:** license agreements, patent families, licensed products, sales, net-sales deductions, territories, royalty rates, minimums, sublicenses, reports, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Patent license library
- Patent family registry
- Licensed product mapping
- Licensee sales ingestion
- Gross sales validation
- Net-sales deduction audit
- Territory eligibility
- Royalty rate calculation
- Per-unit calculation
- Minimum guarantee
- Sublicense income share
- Royalty report audit
- Licensee examination workflow
- Cash reconciliation
- Patent product analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4740>. API: `5740`.
